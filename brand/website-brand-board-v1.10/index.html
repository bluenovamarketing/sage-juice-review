01

Brand direction

Refined without feeling distant

Sage Juice Co. balances elevated editorial design with the ease and welcome of a neighborhood destination. The visual system is composed, tactile, and quietly distinctive.

Generous space, sculptural typography, warm mineral color, and natural material cues create a premium experience without becoming formal or overstated.

02

Identity system

A consistent mark across every setting

Primary artwork

The supplied wordmark remains the authoritative identity reference. Blue Nova will prepare the approved production-ready vector and raster formats.

Required variants

Full color, one-color dark, one-color white, horizontal, compact, social avatar, favicon, print, and signage-ready files.

Usage principle

Preserve clear space and proportion. Avoid distortion, shadows, outlines, unapproved recoloring, or placement over visually active imagery.

05

Motion and imagery

Soft focus, clear atmosphere

Look for

Warm directional light, natural stone and wood, glass, quiet botanical details, thoughtful hands, crafted product moments, and authentic neighborhood texture.

Protect

Concept imagery remains softened or partially obscured until authentic finished-location photography is available and approved.

Avoid

Over-saturated produce collages, generic wellness clichés, clinical imagery, fabricated product details, or visuals that imply an unverified completed space.

06

Brand voice

Warm, assured, and intentionally concise

Inviting

Make the audience feel welcomed into an experience, not marketed at.

Sensory

Use concrete language about freshness, material, place, texture, and ritual.

Composed

Keep messages clean and confident. Let the design provide the sense of occasion.

Responsible

Avoid medical claims, exaggerated exclusivity, and details that have not been verified.
