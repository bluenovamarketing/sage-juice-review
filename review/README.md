# Sage Juice design review hub

The review hub presents only the current client-facing artifacts:

- Brand Foundation v1.11 internally, shown without a public version label.
- Coming Soon Home v1.20 internally, shown without a public version label.
- Dedicated 390px phone and 768px tablet previews.

The Brand Foundation compares Champagne + client-supplied Botanical Sage against Warm Stone + Sage Olive, uses supplied Montage Serif with Manrope, records the full-word underline as selected, and documents imagery, voice, verified social identities, and planned final deliverables. The landing page preserves the approximately eleven-second opening and continuing atmospheric movement, starts a continuous ambient music bed with the motion, waits for one clean entry interaction when browser autoplay policy requires it, opens the simplified closeable **Something fresh is coming** prompt immediately after the final motion frame, keeps the responsive required-field popup form and approved product line, reduces the public page to one hero and one top-right CTA, and gives the supplied address a prominent treatment.

The review hub is a static presentation. Its forms do not store or transmit information. No live website or account change is represented by this review surface.
